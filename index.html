Contradiction Word Solitaire

Support

Contradiction Word Solitaire is a card game. Tap the table card that contradicts your open card and clear the table before time runs out.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: topalserthan@gmail.com

Please include your device model and the level number if you report a problem.

Common questions

How do lives work? You can hold up to 5 lives. A new life arrives every 3 minutes. You lose a life after 3 wrong cards in a level, when time runs out and you retry or leave, or when you restart or leave a level.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Can I remove ads? Any ad-free option will be listed in the app when available.
