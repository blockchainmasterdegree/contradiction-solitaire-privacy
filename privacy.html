Privacy Policy

Contradiction Word Solitaire · Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Contradiction Word Solitaire app ("the app") uses. The app is published by Serthan Topal ("we").

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, jokers, lives and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
